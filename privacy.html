Privacy Policy & Permission Transparency

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Applies to Sauvenza — Crash-Proof Tab & Session Manager (v1.2.1, Extension ID: lnadpeimmaacgdhgekefdcbboafdhaam).

1. Core Local-First Commitment

Sauvenza is engineered so that your browsing activity stays strictly under your physical control. All open tabs, window layouts, Chrome Tab Groups, automatic crash snapshots, and named workspaces are stored exclusively on your device inside Google Chrome's local IndexedDB (SauvenzaDB) and chrome.storage.local.

  • Zero Telemetry or Analytics: Sauvenza includes no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no behavioral tracking pixels.
  • No Cloud Account Required: You never create an account or upload your tab history to use Sauvenza Free or Pro.
  • Incognito Exclusion: Sauvenza explicitly ignores Incognito windows (win.incognito === true). Incognito tabs are never captured, snapshotted, or written to disk.
  • No Data Selling or Sharing: We never sell, rent, trade, or transfer user data to third parties or data brokers.

2. Chrome Web Store Data Disclosures Explained

To comply with Google's Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, Sauvenza declares the data categories touched by its features. Here is exactly what each disclosure means in practice:

Web History (Local Device Only): Chrome classifies open tab URLs and page titles read via the tabs API as "Web history." Sauvenza reads this metadata solely to build local crash-recovery snapshots and workspaces inside your browser. Your URLs and tab titles are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Personally Identifiable Information (Optional Pro License Only): If you choose to purchase or activate a Pro key, your email address is used only to deliver your license key or respond to your support request.
  • Financial & Payment Information (Optional Pro Checkout Only): When upgrading to Pro, card payments are processed externally on Gumroad, or you may submit a manual payment verification form (plan, payment method, amount, transaction reference, and reply email) to the developer. Sauvenza never stores credit card numbers inside the extension.

3. Technical Breakdown of Chrome Permissions

4. Minimal Network Communication (Pro Key Verification)

If you use Sauvenza Free without activating a license key, the extension makes zero network requests of its own.

If you activate a Pro license key, Sauvenza communicates with the developer's Google Apps Script license server once upon activation and approximately once every 24 hours thereafter. The request payload contains only:

  • Your normalized Pro license key (e.g., ANNUAL-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX or LIFETIME-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX)
  • A randomly generated, non-identifiable per-browser UUID (deviceId) used to enforce the one-browser-per-key limit
  • The installed Sauvenza version string (e.g., 1.2.1)

No open tabs, URLs, workspace names, IP-based tracking profiles, or browsing histories are ever included in license verification requests.

5. Data Retention, Export & Deletion Rights

Because all session data lives on your device, you have complete control over retention and deletion at all times:

  • Export Anytime: Export all workspaces and snapshots as portable JSON, Markdown, Plain Text, or CSV from Vault → Import / Export.
  • Automatic Retention Rules: Configure automatic cleanup intervals (7, 30, 60, or 90 days) in Vault → Storage & Retention.
  • Instant Local Wipe: Use Reset all local data in Storage & Retention, or uninstall Sauvenza from chrome://extensions to permanently erase all local extension storage.
